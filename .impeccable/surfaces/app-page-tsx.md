Scope: homepage gateway (`app/page.tsx`), Persuade mode.

- Audience/job: people seeking practical, mostly free tools need to compare the
  available apps and open the right one quickly.
- Action/proof: open an app first; bookmark the app or gateway next; understand
  that one credit balance works across every app and purchases live at
  `credits.34cats.com`.
- Direction: The Familiar Workspace—a polished conventional portal calibrated
  against ChatGPT and Claude. Warm-neutral canvas, white working surfaces,
  restrained 34cats orange, familiar cards and controls, direct language.
- Memorable moment: the app collection is immediately legible and useful; the
  interface recedes rather than performing a visual metaphor.
- Constraints: preserve the 34cats logo and orange, truthful app details,
  privacy obligations, working routes, keyboard access, reduced motion, and
  responsive reading order. Do not copy reference branding or proprietary UI.
- Unresolved: the credits subdomain may not yet expose a balance API, so the
  gateway links to it without displaying a fabricated balance.
