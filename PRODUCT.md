# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are people looking for practical web tools. The products are
free-first, while leaving room for paid offerings for users who find enough
value in them.

## Product Purpose

34cats Apps is the gateway to a growing collection of small web applications
that make recurring tasks quicker, clearer, or less repetitive. It also gives
Sean a practical way to learn as a developer by building, releasing,
maintaining, and improving real products.

Success means the tools are useful to people, remain operational after launch,
and create opportunities to learn from real use. Products may offer paid usage
through a shared 34cats credit balance, but usefulness is not conditional on
payment.

## Positioning

Small, practical apps that stay maintained and improve through real use. They
are working products rather than one-off demos, while also serving as Sean's
ongoing development practice.

## Operating Context

Visitors discover the available apps at apps.34cats.com, then use products
hosted either within this site or on their own 34cats subdomains. The current
collection includes PawPress CMS and Resumeow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site indexes 34cats applications, provides shared account access, and
  hosts product information and legal pages.
- Apps that grow beyond a landing page may move to their own repository and
  subdomain.
- Current products include a headless CMS for Next.js projects and a resume
  builder that produces typeset PDFs.
- Offerings are primarily free tools. One credit balance is intended to work
  across all 34cats apps, with purchases handled at `credits.34cats.com`.
- Product and legal claims must stay factual and must not imply unverified
  customers, results, pricing, or guarantees.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the 34cats name and existing logo assets. The product voice is direct,
practical, honest, and grounded in what the tools currently do. Use a polished,
conventional product interface at the craft level of ChatGPT and Claude without
copying their brands, assets, or proprietary layouts.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing product descriptions and capability details in `config/apps.ts` and
  `app/pawpress-cms/page.tsx`.
- Existing 34cats logo assets in `public/`.
- Existing privacy policy and terms of service in `app/privacy-policy/` and
  `app/terms-of-service/`.
- No testimonials, customer logos, usage benchmarks, or validated pricing
  claims are currently present; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Start with an ordinary task that can be made easier.
- Make tools useful without requiring payment by default.
- Ship in small steps and learn from real use.
- Treat launch as the start of maintenance, not the finish.
- Describe capabilities and outcomes honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide accessible web experiences and comply with applicable personal data
protection and privacy requirements. Collect and process only the personal data
needed to operate each product.
